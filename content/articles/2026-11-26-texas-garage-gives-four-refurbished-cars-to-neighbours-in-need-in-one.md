A Texas garage has given four refurbished cars to local people without transport, all on the same day. The handover takes the number of vehicles given away this year by a project called Autos for Love to eight.

The scheme is run by Dane and Cathy Bailey, who own Auto Experts of Colleyville. Their original plan was modest: do up one gently used car a year, donated by someone in the community, and present it to a neighbour who needed it.

That plan soon outgrew itself. Having seen how recipients reacted, the Baileys wanted to find more cars and more people to help. "One was not enough," says Cathy, while Dane adds that once you have experienced the giving, you want more. Word spread, donated vehicles began arriving in greater numbers, and the couple can now give away several cars a year.

This year they went looking for the people in town who needed wheels most. Friends put forward Barbara Farison, a mother whose car had been totalled when another driver ran a red light.

She had never managed to replace it. For three years, Farison and her children walked whenever no other transport was available, including on the way to school and to buy groceries. It left them at the mercy of the elements, and on one occasion she narrowly avoided becoming the victim of a violent crime.

Her friends contacted the Baileys, who then rang Farison and asked her to come to the shop for what she believed was an interview. Instead, she was given the keys to a 2005 Acura MDX.

Farison said she could hardly put into words how much easier the car would make life for her children and herself, and that it would make her very happy as a mother. Three other people also drove away in a car that day, a cracking result for the Baileys and their generous neighbours.

## Why it matters

Without a car, everyday routines such as getting children to school or buying food can take far more time and effort, as Farison's three years on foot show. Projects like this one demonstrate how donated vehicles, skilled mechanics and neighbours who speak up for one another can add up to practical, life-changing help.
