Motorists in Houston can now book their cars in with high-school students, after a training garage opened to the public in the city's Fifth Ward.

The shop belongs to the Barbara Jordan Career Center, part of Houston Independent School District. Students on the automotive pathway do the work themselves, including oil changes, tyre rotations and multi-point inspections. They are also ready to tinker with whatever is causing a strange sound under the bonnet.

It is open every Friday from 9am to 3pm, and appointments can be made online. Customers bring their own parts and fluids and pay for the students' labour. That is not the most convenient arrangement for a driver, but it does mean each visit supports young people training to become mechanics.

The career center serves 12 school campuses and more than 1,200 students. It aims to line up well-paid, practical jobs and internships in northeast Houston for pupils preparing for in-demand trades and professions. Staff had been working towards opening the garage since the summer.

The drive for extra opportunities follows a finding by a local education group that fewer than 20 per cent of the district's graduates were earning a living wage six years after leaving school. The district's automotive programme has long hoped to give students more chances to develop what it calls soft skills as well as technical know-how, so that they are more attractive to employers.

Cars are only one strand of the offer. The district's technical education programmes also take in the trades, culinary arts, engineering and robotics, and human services, which is a rather broad menu for a school system.

## Why it matters

Practical experience is widely seen as a valuable partner to classroom study in skilled trades, where much of the learning happens with tools in hand. In a district where a local group found fewer than one in five graduates earning a living wage six years on, a shop that puts students to work on real vehicles for real customers offers a practical way to build the skills the automotive programme is aiming for.
