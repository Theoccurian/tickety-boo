A 61-year-old from West Sussex has won the first bodybuilding contest he ever entered, and the result has brought him an invitation to the world championships in Seville.

Ian Fox, from Selsey, took first place in the class for competitors aged over 50 at the PCA First Timers event in Derby. It was a cracking result for a debut. The championships in Spain are due to be held later this week.

Mr Fox says he has always kept himself in good shape. It was after he joined Imperium Strength Fitness, a gym in Chichester, this year that people there encouraged him to try a competition.

He admits the early stages felt daunting. He had never done anything like it and was unsure what was involved, but he reflects that you pick things up as you go along. He is full of thanks for his coach, Marcelo Prado, who backed him throughout his preparation.

He and his wife plan to make the journey to Seville together, and he feels the chance is too good to pass up.

Even so, he regards the trip to Spain as a bonus. His main aim is to change how people think about what those in their sixties are capable of. He believes that the way you think about yourself shapes who you become, and that defining yourself by your age is a mistake.

For Mr Fox, the story is about far more than bodybuilding. He sees it as a call to keep setting goals, to find the nerve to try something entirely new, and to refuse to let a birthday decide what is possible.

## Why it matters

Many people quietly assume that bold new challenges are for the young, and that belief can stop them from ever trying. Staying active and strong is widely recognised as a good way to support health and independence in later life. A first-time competitor winning his category at 61 is a welcome reminder that it is rarely too late to take up something new, and that a supportive gym and a good coach can help along the way.
